Buying, delivery & contact

The Glass Element is a private collection of cameras, lenses and eyewear based in Thailand. Each page describes one item or an explicitly stated kit, with photographs and a listed asking price.

Buy through the linked Shopee listing

For products with a Shopee button, that link opens our listing at second_hand_japan. Check the selected option, current availability, final price and the shipping and return terms shown at checkout. Promotions can change. Product descriptions and dated prices on this site are snapshots, not a live inventory feed.

Direct enquiries

Email contact@theglasselement.com with the exact product name or page link, your questions and destination country/postcode. Confirm that you have received a reply before making a payment. Direct-enquiry asking prices are identified separately where they differ from a Shopee promotion.

Condition and what is included

Used items may have cosmetic or functional wear. Repair and untested items have a visible label and specific disclosures. Read the contents list: lenses, straps, chargers and cases shown in photographs are included only when the listing says so. Ask for a current functional check or close-ups when needed.

Delivery, payment and returns

Shopee orders follow the options and terms available on the actual Shopee order. For a direct transaction, delivery coverage, charges, payment arrangements and any return terms must be agreed with the seller before payment. No universal delivery, return period or warranty is promised by this catalogue.
