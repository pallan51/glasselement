Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our curation and operations.

Where is The Glass Element located?

The Glass Element is based in Thailand. We curate, restore, and field-test our vintage eyeglasses and exotic cameras across tropical Southeast Asia before they find their new owners.

What kind of eyewear do you sell?

We specialize in rare, archived, and vintage eyewear from premium Japanese brands such as Masunaga, Kaneko Optical, Eyevan, and Oliver Peoples, often upgrading them with premium CR39 optical lenses.
