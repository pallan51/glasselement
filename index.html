A few useful details

Before you buy

What currency are the prices in?

Prices are individually labelled USD or THB. Shopee prices were checked on 25 October 2026 and can change with promotions and selected options. Existing USD asking prices are preserved. Price sorting groups items by currency. Confirm the final total and delivery costs before buying.

Can I see more photographs?

Galleries identify original photographs and photographs from our Shopee listings. Follow the Shopee link for more views and full listing details, or ask for current close-ups before buying.

How do I check fit, condition and accessories?

Ask for the frame measurements and lens details for eyewear, or the menu language, tested functions and included accessories for a camera. Confirm any wear or defects before agreeing a purchase.

Where are the items located, and can you ship to me?

The collection is based in Thailand. Include your country and postcode in your enquiry so delivery or collection options can be discussed.
